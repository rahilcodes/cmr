Rewards & Recognition

Celebrating talent, effort, and excellence across every CMR institution. Every participant gains visibility, recognition, and growth opportunities.

🏆 Category Winners

Each category in every college will have:

1 Winner
1 Runner-up

🎯 Total Winners

With 10 categories per college, there will be 20 winners from each college, ensuring fair representation for everyone.

🎤 On-Stage Recognition

Winners will be felicitated on stage in the auditorium during the official results announcement ceremony.

🌟 Digital Recognition

Selected winners and outstanding participants will be featured on official CMR and BeSURE social media platforms.

🎓 Certificates

Winners receive Certificates of Excellence, while all participants receive participation certificates.

🤝 Mentorship

Top performers may receive mentorship and guidance from industry experts and professionals.
