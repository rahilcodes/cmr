Talent Categories

Choose one or more categories that best represent your skills, creativity, innovation, or impact. Each category has clear examples to guide your submission.

CMR Athlete Prodigy

Sports performance, fitness excellence, and competitive gameplay.

  • Match highlights or training clips
  • Fitness transformations or workouts
  • Indoor or outdoor sports performance
  • Athletic drills or challenges
  • Sports achievements or medals

Tech Maker Award

Technology-based innovation, coding, and engineering.

  • Coding projects or demos
  • AI / ML tools or automations
  • Robotics or hardware builds
  • App or website development
  • Technical problem-solving

Creative Storyteller

Visual storytelling through editing and narrative creativity.

  • Short films or cinematic edits
  • Video editing showreels
  • Digital storytelling concepts
  • Creative reels
  • Concept-based visuals

Voice of CMR

Performance-based expression using voice or movement.

  • Singing performances
  • Rapping or spoken word
  • Poetry recitation
  • Public speaking or speeches
  • Dancing performances

Startup Spark

Entrepreneurship, innovation, and business thinking.

  • Startup ideas or pitch videos
  • Working prototypes or MVPs
  • Revenue or traction showcase
  • Business model explanation
  • Product or service demos

Digital Influence Champion

Creators who educate, inspire, or engage audiences.

  • Educational content
  • Lifestyle or niche-based content
  • Personal branding
  • Informative reels or series
  • Consistent creator portfolios

Innovation & IP Master

Research-driven and innovation-focused contributions.

  • Patent filings or concepts
  • Research projects
  • Scientific experiments
  • Technical papers or models
  • Innovative problem-solving

Social Changemaker

Positive impact and meaningful contribution to society.

  • Community service initiatives
  • Awareness campaigns
  • NGO or volunteer work
  • Social problem-solving
  • Impact-driven storytelling

Visual Artist

Creative expression through art and visual media.

  • Photography portfolios
  • Videography projects
  • Drone shots
  • Digital or handmade artwork
  • Creative compositions

Campus Icon

Personality, presence, and overall campus influence.

  • Modeling or fashion shoots
  • Confidence & stage presence
  • Personality-based reels
  • Style and presentation
  • Campus representation content
