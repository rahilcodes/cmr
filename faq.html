Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about participation, eligibility, submissions, evaluation, and results for CMR Talent League 2025.

1. Who can participate in CMR Talent League? +

Only current students of CMR Group of Institutions (UG, PG, Diploma) are eligible to participate.

2. Can I participate in more than one category? +

Yes. You may participate in multiple categories, but each category requires a separate submission.

3. Is team participation allowed? +

Yes, teams of up to 5 members are allowed except for identity-based categories like Campus Icon.

4. How do I register? +

You must fill the official registration form. After registration, you will be added to the WhatsApp group.

5. How will I submit my entry? +

You need to upload your content on a public social media account and submit the link using the submission form.

6. Which platforms are allowed? +

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and long YouTube videos (for tech/research categories).

7. Can I upload multiple posts for one category? +

No. Only one final submission per category will be evaluated.

8. Should my account be public? +

Yes. Your account must remain public until the results are announced.

9. Are hashtags mandatory? +

Yes. You must use the official hashtags provided in the submission guidelines.

10. Can I edit or delete my post later? +

No. Editing or deleting posts before results may lead to disqualification.

11. What languages are allowed? +

English, Hindi, and Telugu are allowed.

12. Are subtitles compulsory? +

Subtitles are recommended but not mandatory.

13. How will winners be selected? +

Evaluation is based on authenticity, skill, creativity, engagement, and growth potential.

14. Who are the judges? +

Judging is done by Team BeSURE, industry experts, and CMR higher management.

15. When will results be announced? +

Results will be announced on stage and shared on official social media platforms.

16. What rewards do winners receive? +

Winners receive certificates, recognition, mentorship opportunities, and digital features.

17. Will all participants get certificates? +

Yes, all valid participants will receive participation certificates.

18. Is there any registration fee? +

No. Participation in CMR Talent League is completely free.

19. What happens if rules are violated? +

Violation of rules may lead to disqualification without prior notice.

20. Whom should I contact for help? +

You can contact the organizing team via the official Contact page or WhatsApp group.
