Event Overview

What is CMR Talent League?

A 30-day digital talent showcase where CMR students create content demonstrating skills, publish it publicly, tag official pages, and compete in curated categories. The campaign promotes authentic achievements, innovation, sports, arts, and creativity.

30 Days · Fully Digital · Campus-wide
How it works:
→ Choose your category → Create and publish content
→ Tag official CMR + BeSure pages → Compete, learn, and grow

Participation Rules

Simple, clear, and fair

Eligibility & Format
  • Active CMR student only.
  • Individual or team participation (except personal categories).
  • Content must be posted from public accounts.
Tagging & Hashtags
  • Must tag official CMR + BeSure pages.
  • Must use hashtags:
  • #CMRTalentLeague
  • #PoweredByBeSure
  • #CMRHyderabad
  • #LifeAtCMR
  • Categories must be mentioned in caption.
Integrity & Safety
  • Disqualification for fake engagement, plagiarism, vulgarity, harassment, or copyrighted media.
