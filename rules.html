Rules & Guidelines

Please read all rules carefully before participating. These guidelines ensure fairness, transparency, and equal opportunity for all students.

Eligibility

  • Only current students of CMR Group of Institutions are eligible.
  • UG, PG, and Diploma students may participate.
  • Alumni, faculty members, and external participants are not allowed.
  • Participants may enter multiple categories.
  • Each category requires a separate submission.

Team Participation Rules

  • Maximum of 5 members per team.
  • Only one member needs to upload the content.
  • All team members must be tagged or mentioned.
  • Certificates will be issued individually.
  • The team leader is responsible for coordination.

Submission Rules

  • Content must be posted on a public account.
  • Accepted platforms: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook.
  • YouTube long-form videos are allowed for tech/startup/research categories.
  • Mandatory tagging of official CMR & BeSURE pages.
  • Mandatory hashtags must be used.
  • Posts must remain public until results are announced.

Content Guidelines

  • Vertical video format (9:16) is preferred.
  • Reels / Shorts: 30–90 seconds.
  • YouTube videos: up to 10 minutes.
  • Languages allowed: English, Hindi, Telugu.
  • Subtitles are strongly recommended.
  • Images allowed for art, photography & research categories.

Judging Criteria

  • 20% – Authenticity
  • 25% – Skill & execution
  • 20% – Organic reach & engagement
  • 20% – Creativity & presentation
  • 15% – Growth potential

Disqualification Conditions

  • Buying likes, views, or followers.
  • Plagiarized or copyrighted content.
  • Fake achievements or misleading information.
  • Abusive, hateful, or inappropriate content.
  • Private or deleted posts during evaluation.
  • Any content harming the reputation of CMR or BeSURE.
