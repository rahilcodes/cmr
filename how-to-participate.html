How to Participate

Follow these simple steps to successfully participate in the CMR Talent League 2025. Make sure you complete each step carefully to ensure your entry is valid.

01

Register

Fill out the official registration form to enroll in the competition. Once registered, you will be added to the official WhatsApp group.

02

Join WhatsApp Group

All updates, announcements, and the submission link will be shared only through the official WhatsApp group.

03

Create Your Content

Choose your category and prepare your content according to the guidelines provided for that category.

04

Post on Social Media

Upload your content on Instagram, YouTube, or Facebook with required tags and hashtags. Your account must be public.

05

Submit Your Link

Paste your post link in the submission form shared via WhatsApp or email.

06

Evaluation & Results

Entries will be evaluated by industry experts and CMR management. Winners will be announced on stage and online.

Important Participation Notes

  • Only students of CMR Group of Institutions are eligible.
  • You may participate in more than one category.
  • Each category requires a separate submission.
  • Team participation is allowed (up to 5 members).
  • Posts must remain public until results are announced.
  • Any form of plagiarism or fake engagement leads to disqualification.
  • Follow the official posting format and hashtag guidelines.
